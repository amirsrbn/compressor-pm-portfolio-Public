# SGT-400 Turbo-Compressor Digital Twin & Predictive Maintenance

**Independent Engineering Research & Development Portfolio**

**Developed by Amir Sarebanzadeh**  
Electronics Engineer | Industrial Instrumentation & Control Systems | Digital Twin Development

This repository presents selected public components and documented results from my independently developed SGT-400 turbo-compressor digital twin and predictive-maintenance research platform.

The ongoing project combines physics-based dynamic modelling, gas turbine and LP/HP compressor simulation, industrial control systems, fault injection, machine-learning-based equipment monitoring, and industrial software integration.

Development activities span MATLAB/Simulink, Python, OPC-UA communication, Ignition Perspective HMI, and automated verification and testing, including simulation scenarios for a virtual fleet of 15 compressor units.

**Project status:** Active research and development. Individual components have undergone development testing, while end-to-end system validation remains in progress. The platform has not been deployed as an operational industrial system.

This public portfolio contains selected materials and historical experimental results. It does not represent the complete private engineering repository or claim final qualification of the integrated platform.
